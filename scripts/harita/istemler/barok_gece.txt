Image 1 is a finished bird's-eye map painted as one Baroque painting in the manner of Caravaggio and Rembrandt, lit by golden late-afternoon light. Repaint exactly the same picture at night. Keep the composition identical: the road, every round plaza, building, tree, figure and object stays in exactly the same place, size and shape; nothing is added, removed or moved. The painting style stays the same: glossy Baroque oil paint, deep warm shadows.

Only the light changes, as in Rembrandt's night scenes: the golden daylight beam is gone; the sky becomes a deep night blue with a pale moon and a few stars behind dark clouds; the land falls into deeper shadow. Instead, small warm lights glow everywhere: candlelight in the windows, torches and lanterns along the road, a lantern beside each round plaza so every plaza lies in its own warm pool of light and stays clearly visible. The figures in the hidden scenes are lit by candles and torches, like Caravaggio's and Rembrandt's paintings.

The round plazas stay empty. The flat grey margins on both sides (if any) continue the same night scenery. No text, letters, numbers, watermark, frames or borders.
