Image 1 is a finished bird's-eye map of the 18th century, from a Rococo pleasure garden through the English countryside to the Panthéon in Paris, in daylight. Repaint exactly the same picture at night, as a Rococo masked ball night. Keep the composition identical: the road, all three round plazas, every building, figure, tree and field stay in exactly the same place, size and shape; nothing is added, removed or moved except light. Keep the painting styles of each zone.
