The result is a tall vertical (1:3) bird's-eye storybook map of one art-historical period: the Baroque, a journey north from Rome to Delft.

{{YERLESIM}}

The picture has five zones from bottom to top, each about one fifth of its height, with one plaza in the middle of each: Rome (two zones), Madrid, Antwerp, Delft. Paint the whole picture edge to edge as one continuous landscape. Nothing of the flat sketch may remain: no blank paper background, no flat beige strip, no vignettes or framed scenes, no clouds separating the zones. The zones flow into each other.

The road is a real painted road whose surface changes with each zone (travertine paving in Rome, a dusty track in Castile, cobbles in Flanders, brick along a Dutch canal), but its position and width never change.

Each of the {{N}} ovals becomes a small, round, empty paved plaza, flat and at ground level like the road (not a raised disc, step or pedestal). Nothing stands on a plaza: no animals, objects or people (a small statue will be placed there later). The period structure named for that level stands right beside its plaza.

The world is made of famous Baroque paintings merged into one land. Each hidden detail stays next to its own level and appears only once. The whole picture shares one consistent warm light and a gentle bird's-eye perspective, with a touch of the period: golden late-afternoon light with soft dramatic shadows, rich warm colours, billowing clouds lit from within as on a Baroque ceiling, and a broad Dutch sky at the top. Use a light, bright palette with clear daylight; the picture must not look dark or varnished, even where the paintings themselves are dark. Keep it calm and uncluttered: each hidden detail is one small scene, and at least half of the picture is quiet open space (sky, hills, fields, water, paved squares) with no figures or buildings. No crowds, no extra scenes, no extra buildings beyond those listed.

From bottom to top:
1 (lowest plaza): Rome. Beside the plaza, the façade and great dome of Saint Peter's Basilica with Bernini's curved colonnades embracing an oval square. Hidden nearby, in the sky above the dome: Apollo driving his golden chariot across golden clouds, led by Aurora scattering flowers and surrounded by the dancing Hours in pastel robes (Guido Reni, Aurora).
2: Rome, a narrow street of ochre houses in deep shadow and sharp sunlight. Beside the plaza, the façade of the church of San Luigi dei Francesi. Hidden nearby, in a small tavern whose front is open to the street: men in plumed hats and doublets sitting at a table counting coins, Christ and Saint Peter standing at the right, Christ pointing, a diagonal beam of light falling across the wall (Caravaggio, The Calling of Saint Matthew).
3: Madrid, a dry golden Castilian plain. Beside the plaza, the royal Alcázar palace with its slate spires. Hidden nearby, seen through a large open side of a palace room: the little blonde Infanta Margarita in a wide pale silver dress with her maids of honour, a dwarf, a big sleeping dog, and the painter standing at his huge canvas, a man in a lit doorway at the back (Velázquez, Las Meninas).
4: Antwerp. Beside the plaza, the Cathedral of Our Lady with its tall lacy white spire, and Rubens's house with its Baroque portico. Hidden nearby, on a small hill outside the town: muscular men straining to raise the cross diagonally against the sky, a dog in the corner (Rubens, The Elevation of the Cross).
5 (top plaza): Delft. Beside the plaza, the city of Delft seen across calm water: brick walls, gates with twin towers, red roofs and a church spire under a huge cloudy sky (Vermeer, View of Delft). Hidden nearby, coming out of a city gate in a pool of light: a militia company marching forward, the captain in black with a red sash, the lieutenant in pale gold, lances, a flag, a drummer and a small girl in gold (Rembrandt, The Night Watch). A windmill on the flat land beyond.

The top and bottom edges fade into soft mist so the map can join the neighbouring periods. No text, letters, numbers or watermark. No frames or borders. No modern objects. Small figures may appear inside the hidden scenes, but no large characters stand on the road.
