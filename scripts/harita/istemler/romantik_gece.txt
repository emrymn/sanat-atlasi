Image 1 is a finished bird's-eye map of the 19th century from Romanticism to Realism, in warm late-afternoon light: a misty forest with a Gothic ruin and a wanderer on a crag at the bottom, London on the Thames with the Palace of Westminster and a fire, a green Pre-Raphaelite stream with Ophelia and a Gothic chapel, Paris with the Opéra Garnier, and the village of Barbizon with gleaners at the top. Repaint exactly the same picture at night. Keep the composition identical: the road, all five round plazas, every building, figure, tree, river and field stay in exactly the same place, size and shape; nothing is added, removed or moved except light. Keep the painting style.

Only the lighting changes. The whole map lies under one shared, calm blue-silver moonlight with a deep night-blue sky, a bright moon and stars, so the picture stays one harmonious painting; the warm lights below are small glows inside that same moonlit night, never separate bright patches:
- The misty forest at the bottom: the Gothic ruin and the sea of mist glow pale silver under the moon, as in Friedrich's moonlit paintings; the wanderer is a dark silhouette against the moonlit mist.
- London: warm gas lamps line the bridge and the embankment and glow in the windows of the Palace of Westminster; the clock face is lit; the fire across the river still glows red-orange and is reflected in the dark water.
- The Pre-Raphaelite stream: moonlight on the water and the willows, a few tiny fireflies among the flowers, a soft warm glow in the chapel windows.
- Paris: the Opéra is festively lit like a gala night, its windows and gilded statues glowing warmly, gas lamps along the boulevard and lit windows in the buildings.
- Barbizon at the top: warm candlelight in the farmhouse windows; the fields and haystacks lie silvery in the moonlight.
- The road is softly lit along its length by small lamps, and a lamp stands beside each round plaza so that all five plazas lie in their own soft pool of light and stay clearly visible.

The round plazas stay empty: no fountain, water, figure or object on them. The flat grey margins on both sides (if any) continue the same night scenery. No text, letters, numbers, watermark, frames or borders.
