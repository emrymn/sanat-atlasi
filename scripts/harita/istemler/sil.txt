Image 1 is a finished bird's-eye map painted as one Baroque painting in the manner of Caravaggio and Rembrandt (deep warm shadows, golden theatrical light, glossy oil paint). The mask opens only three small areas; everything else is locked and must not change.

Each open area contains a round paved plaza and the short paved path that joins it to the main road. Remove the plaza and its short path completely, and repaint the area as the surrounding scenery continuing naturally, in the same style and light: dark grass, shrubs, earth and shadow, matching what is around it. The main road beside each area stays exactly as it is, with an unbroken edge where the path used to join it.

No plaza, path, paving, figure or new building in the open areas. No text, letters, numbers, watermark, frames or borders.
