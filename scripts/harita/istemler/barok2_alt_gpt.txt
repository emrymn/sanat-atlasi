Image 1 is the lower half of a tall bird's-eye storybook map painted in the manner of Claude Lorrain and Poussin: the gardens of Versailles with their parterres and fountains, a round empty paved plaza on the left, Poussin's shepherds at a tomb, and a pale winding road. The mask opens only the lowest strip of the picture; everything above it is locked and must not change.

In the open strip, remove the sea, the harbour, the ships and the boats completely. The winding road that comes down from the gardens continues without any break through the open strip, with the same width, curve and pale paving, and leaves the picture at the bottom centre. Around the road paint a calm, sunlit pastoral landscape in the manner of Claude Lorrain that flows seamlessly out of the gardens above it, with no horizon line, border or change of scale where the strip begins: green meadows, feathery trees, a few grazing sheep, soft golden evening light. Keep the classical round temple at the left beside the road.

No water, sea, ships or boats in the open strip. No new plaza. No text, letters, numbers, watermark, frames or borders.
