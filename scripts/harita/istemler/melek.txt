Image 1 is a finished bird's-eye map of the Middle Ages painted in medieval styles. The mask opens only the empty burnished gold-leaf areas in the middle and one corner at the bottom left; everything else is locked and must not change.

In the gold areas: the whole open area becomes burnished gold leaf with fine punched patterns (the green hill and the small green trees standing in the gold are removed and become gold, as in a Sienese gold-ground panel), and add small Sienese angels in the manner of Duccio and Simone Martini (see the other images), flying in graceful pairs and small groups, with rose, pale blue and green robes, coloured wings and punched gold halos. Keep them small and scattered so the gold no longer looks empty; leave some gold visible between them.

In the bottom-left corner: remove the large standing friar and the donkey and repaint that corner as the surrounding rocky hillside in the style of a Giotto fresco (matte chalky colours, simplified stepped rocks).

No text, letters, numbers, watermark, frames or borders.
