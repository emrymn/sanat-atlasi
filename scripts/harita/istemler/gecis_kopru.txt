Image 1 is one tall picture made of two paintings of a continuous storybook map with an empty flat grey band between them. At the bottom is the top edge of the Renaissance map: green Italian hills, a hill town, a triumphal arch and a pale stone road coming up from below. At the top is the bottom edge of the Baroque map: a dark, golden, candle-lit Baroque world with St Peter's dome, a colonnade and the same pale stone road continuing upwards. Do not change the top and bottom paintings at all.

Paint only the empty grey band in the middle: a calm, wide river of time flows across it from left to right, shimmering, with soft mist on the water. The pale stone road coming up from the bottom crosses the river on a stone bridge and continues without any break to meet the road at the top exactly where it enters. The bridge itself shows the passage between the two periods: its lower half (on the Renaissance side) is built in calm, harmonious Renaissance style, with plain round arches and pale stone; as it crosses it becomes richer Baroque, with curving balustrades, statues and gilded details. The riverbanks are Renaissance green hills and cypresses on the lower side and Baroque gardens with fountains on the upper side; the light changes from bright day at the bottom to warm golden evening at the top. The band joins both paintings with no visible seam, border or line.

No plaza, no figures on the road, no frames, no text, letters, numbers or watermark.
