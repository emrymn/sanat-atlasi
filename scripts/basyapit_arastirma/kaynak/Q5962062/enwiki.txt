Dawn (French: L'Aurore), also known as the Girl with a Lily, is an oil-on-canvas painting created in 1881 by the French artist William-Adolphe Bouguereau. It was one of his most notable works. Its dimensions are 214.9 × 107 cm. This is the first in a series of canvases representing the four Times of Day: the others were Dusk in 1882; Night in 1883; and Day in 1884.
It is now in the Birmingham Museum of Art.
