**K1 (arşiv katalog) doğrulanan:** "Tiziano / Venere / dipinto / Galleria degli Uffizi / Firenze / (Altezza per Larghezza) 119 x 165 / inv. 1890, 1437 (1890 post)"; Notizie: "giunse a Firenze nel 1631 con l'eredità di Vittoria della Rovere; non identificabile con sicurezza … inventari 1635 e 1638; sicuramente al Poggio Imperiale nel 1654-1655; inventario Guardaroba di Vittoria della Rovere 1656 e ancora 1694."
**K2 (enwiki):** Venus of Urbino / Reclining Venus; oil on canvas; begun 1532 or 1534, perhaps completed 1534, not sold until 1538; Uffizi. Pose from Giorgione Dresden Sleeping Venus (c.1510–11, landscape Titian); indoors, meets gaze, sensuality explicit. Stares viewer not embarrassed; right hand posy roses; other hand over genitals; dog (fidelity); two maids cassone. Commission: Ippolito de' Medici cardinal; 20 Oct 1532 Venice night Angela Zaffetta (courtesan); Titian Ippolito portrait + likely nude Zaffetta; 20 Dec 1534 letter; Ippolito died Aug 1535 never saw. Guidobaldo II della Rovere (24) Jan 1538 sat portrait, keen buy, bought months later, "the nude woman"; inherited Urbino 1538 → name; mostly Pesaro; alternatively commissioned for marriage 1534 Giulia Varano (10)/consummation. Interpretation: courtesan ↔ marriage; Goffen cassone=corredo, hand=conception belief, dog=Eleonora Gonzaga portrait/della Rovere/husband; Hale dog "absurd". Hope "nothing other than beautiful nude … devoid classical/allegorical"; Wind "undisguised hedonism dispelled Platonic metaphors". Masturbation reading linked Renaissance marriage customs (Smarthistory Jewitt 2020) — akademik yorum.
**K3 (itwiki):** 1538; olio su tela; 119×165; Uffizi. Guidobaldo March 1538 urged agent "donna nuda"; asked mother Eleonora money, refused; stayed bottega; pledged pay; months later Urbino; educational "model" for Giulia da Varano (married 1534 political) to persuade connubio amoroso allegorically. Vasari praised only "certi panni sottili"; Milanesi "most beautiful Venus/nude"; favorita portrait no riscontri, model recurs ≥3 canvases (La Bella Urbino). 1631 Vittoria→Firenze; 1635/38 not identifiable; Poggio Imperiale 1654-55; 1694 gallery. Ingres copy 1821 Walters; Verdi reproduction; influence Ingres Odalisque/Goya Maja/Manet Olympia.
