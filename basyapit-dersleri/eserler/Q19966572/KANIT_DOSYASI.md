Title tentative: 1773 "Gallant Scene"; Lipgart "Scene from Boccaccio"; 1800–50 "Mars and Venus"; 1958 "Alexander and Roxane"; Neverov "Zeus and Alcimene"; Hartt "Maid Warning…"; Thornton "Courtesan Receiving a Client". Produced 1524–25 unknown location (left Rome 1524, Mantua 1525); maybe Federico II Gonzaga. Naked man+woman embracing on bed with two erotic bas-reliefs (satyr+goat, satyr+woman); cat under bed (Madonna of the Cat Capodimonte 1522–23); elderly female servant doorway right. Vasari: owned Vespasiano I Gonzaga; Milanese 1880→Berlin version; 1920 Hermitage=original Berlin=copy; Berlin→Sanssouci 1930→Reinsberg 1942→lost WWII. Reiffenstein bought from Thomas Jenkins for Catherine the Great; Hamilton 1780 "best in collection".
