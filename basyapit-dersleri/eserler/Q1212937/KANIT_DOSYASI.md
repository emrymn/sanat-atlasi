**K1 (NG) doğrulanan alanlar:** "Hans Holbein the Younger, 'The Ambassadors', 1533"; Room 4; Framed. "Oil on wood (Baltic/Polish oak, identified)"; "207 × 209.5 cm"; "Bought, 1890"; "NG1314". Sitters: Jean de Dinteville (left, second diplomatic mission for Francis I), Georges de Selve (right, Bishop of Lavaur, close friend). Bağlam: pope refused annul Henry VIII/Catherine of Aragon → break with Rome; 1533 married Anne Boleyn; Dinteville attended wedding on King's behalf; Selve mission exact nature unknown. Signature+date 1533 beneath signature on marble floor behind left figure; unusual elaborate → proud. Skull anamorphic between feet, particular angle; crucifix hidden top left (green damask curtain) → redemption. Top shelf: celestial globe, sundials, torquetum on red Turkish carpet; made by Nicholas Kratzer (Henry VIII royal astronomer). Lower shelf: lute broken string + case face down; arithmetic book open division wedged set square; Lutheran hymn book two non-consecutive pages; flutes one missing → lack harmony; terrestrial globe France + Polisy. Dinteville pink satin tunic + lynx fur black cape; gold tassels dagger gilding. Provenance: Cazillac/Blaigny/Voisin/Lamoignan; Beaujon sale → Lebrun; 1792 engraving Galerie des Peintres Flamands; sold England by 1792; Buchanan → Pleydell; 1890. Frame: ebonised box oak made Gallery 1996, 16th-c N European box frame design; allows side view of skull.
