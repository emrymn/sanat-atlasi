### Konu / figürler / bağlam

**C017** — Afiş Moulin Rouge'un kalabalık dans salonunu ve yıldızlarını reklam eder: "the 'boneless' acrobat Valentin le Désossé and La Goulue, 'the glutton,' whose cancan skirts were lifted at the finale of the chahut" (Met); IMA: "dancing the can-can accompanied by her partner 'No-Bones' Valentin". **A.** K7, K10.
**C018** — La Goulue = Louise Weber'in sahne adı (enwiki, Larousse *Dictionnaire de la Danse* 1999 atfı; NGA "Born Louise Weber"). **B.** K15, K11.
**C019** — Lakap "the Glutton" / "the Greedy One" (obur); kökeni: dans ederken müşteri kadehlerini boşaltması (moulinrouge.fr + Sorrell, *Dance Magazine* 1953 atfı); NGA: "so named for her voracious appetite for all things sensual". **B.** K7, K15, K11, K10.
**C020** — La Goulue doğum/ölüm: 12 Temmuz 1866 Clichy – 29 Ocak 1929 Paris (10th arrondissement); Pantin mezarlığından Montmartre mezarlığına nakil (enwiki infobox + Larousse 1999 atfı). Doğum yılı ihtilaflı → C050. **B.** K15.
**C021** — Valentin le Désossé = Jacques Renaudin (26 Şubat 1843 Paris – 4 Mart 1907 Sceaux); "He **may have been** the son of a notary from Sceaux"; "**it is thought that**… he worked as a wine merchant by day"; gece Moulin Rouge'da dans ettiği kesin; 1895'te sahneden çekildi (Pessis/Crépineau 1990 atfı). Ad ihtilaflı → C049. **B.** K14.
**C022** — Valentin'in profili: uzun ve ince, kemer (aquiline) burun, belirgin çene, uzun kol ve bacaklar; "usually wearing a black suit and a top hat" (Sorrell 1953 atfı). Şapka rengi belirtilmez. **B.** K14.
**C023** — La Goulue chahut/cancan dans eder; etekleri finalde kaldırılır; partneri "No-Bones" Valentin (Met + IMA). **A.** K7, K10.
**C024** — La Goulue Moulin Rouge'un açılışından itibaren yıldız, kalıcı headliner; "Weber decided to part company with the Moulin Rouge in 1895" (Rey 1959 atfı). Ayrılış yılı ihtilaflı → C051. **B.** K15, K10.
**C025** — Ayrılık sonrası: panayır gösterisi yatırımı başarısız; depresyon, ağır içki, servetin tükenmesi; 1929'da ölüm (Rey 1959, Souvais 2008 atıfları). 1928 Montmartre dönüşü + yer fıstığı/sigara/kibrit satışı web/blog düzeyi refs ile; Yaneff 1925 Neuilly-sur-Marne der (C). **Bu ayrıntılar quiz'e alınmadı.** **B.** K15, K13.
**C026** — Moulin Rouge 1889'da boulevard de Clichy'de açıldı ("brassy dance hall and drinking garden"); açılışta Lautrec'in bir tablosu giriş yakınında sergileniyordu; IMA: "opened in Montmartre in 1889". **A.** K7, K10.
**C027** — Kurucular: Charles Zidler ve ortağı Joseph Oller; "late 1889"; karnaval benzeri atmosfer: dansçılar, şarkıcılar, kuklacılar, akrobatlar, hayvan numaraları (NGA). **B.** K11.
**C028** — Met: Lautrec "was commissioned to create the six-foot-tall advertisement that launched his postermaking career and made him famous overnight" (görevlendirme olgusu A; genesis ayrıntısı yok → C048). **A.** K7.
**C029** — NGA: "Despite the fame of this poster, its genesis **remains unclear**. The owner of the Moulin Rouge, Charles Zidler, **may have** approached Lautrec directly, **or** Lautrec **may have** won a competition. It is also **uncertain** who first introduced Lautrec to the medium of lithography, although the artist Pierre Bonnard is **often mentioned as a possibility**." **B.** K11.
**C030** — Lautrec'in ilk afişi: IMA "For his first poster…"; Met "launched his postermaking career"; NGA "initial foray into lithography" (B). **A.** K10, K7, K11.
**C031** — Nüsha sayısı: enwiki (Lee & Robinson, *IMA: Highlights of the Collection* 2005 atfı) "**probably** printed in **about** 3,000 copies"; "Although most examples were pasted as advertising posters and lost, surviving examples are in the collection of the Indianapolis Museum of Art and many other institutions"; NGA "about three thousand". Hedge korundu. **B.** K3, K11.
**C032** — Afiş Lautrec'i bir gecede ünlü yaptı (Met "made him famous overnight"); IMA Gallery Labels: "within days a collected item, within weeks the object of press reviews, within months the partner of paintings in avant-garde exhibitions, and within a year the internationally recognized symbol of 1890s Paris". **A.** K7, K10.
**C033** — Poster tasarımında ilk kez gerçek şovmenler: IMA "for the first time in poster design, Toulouse-Lautrec featured the actual entertainers in the advertisement"; NGA "By advertising a specific celebrity rather than anonymous beauties, Lautrec infused his poster with star power" (B). **A.** K10, K11.
**C034** — Seyirci siluetleri: NGA "a sizable audience, rendered by Lautrec in silhouette form"; "The unusual use of silhouette was influenced by the flat designs of Japanese art then popular among the avant-garde and also by the Chat Noir's shadow theater productions, in which zinc cutouts were back-lit"; NGA modern duyarlılık: "bold colors, a variety of lettering styles, and innovative use of silhouettes"; enwiki (NGA atfı) aynı. **B.** K11, K3.
**C035** — Kabare adı afişte üç kez yinelenir: NGA "The dance hall's name is repeated three times in an eye-catching design that then draws the viewer's eye down to the central motif: La Goulue"; enwiki "triple repetition". Sayı ihtilaflı → C047. **B.** K11, K3.
**C036** — Chéret'nin 1889 "Bal du Moulin Rouge" afişi Moulin Rouge'u reklam eden ilk afişti; Chéret "father of the modern poster"; anonim flörtöz kadın tipi "chérettes"; NGA Lautrec'in afişini "far more daring" olarak karşılaştırır. **B.** K11.
**C037** — Beyaz jupon: enwiki (IMA kitabı atfı) "The stark white of her petticoats, depicted with just a few lines on the white paper, epitomizes Toulouse-Lautrec's boldly simplistic style, a sharp break from the text-heavy posters of the day"; Yaneff "leaving the paper blank" (C destek). **B.** K3, K13.
